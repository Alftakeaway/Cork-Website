Allergen Matrix

Below you'll find the 14 major allergens as defined by UK food law (Natasha's Law) for every dish on our menu. We take allergies seriously — please always inform your server of any allergies or intolerances when ordering, as recipes and suppliers may change.

⚠ Important: This matrix is a guide based on our standard recipes. We handle all 14 allergens in our kitchen and cannot guarantee total absence of cross-contamination. Dishes marked "may contain" indicate potential trace presence from shared equipment or supplier declarations. Always speak to our team about your specific needs — we're happy to talk through ingredients in detail.

Drinks

Wine: Contains sulphites (naturally occurring and added as preservative). Some wines may use egg, milk, or fish derivatives in fining — ask for vegan options.

Beer: Contains gluten (barley/wheat). Gluten-free options available — ask our team.

Spirits & Liqueurs: Generally allergen-free, but some liqueurs contain milk (Bailey's), nuts (Amaretto, Frangelico), or gluten (grain-based spirits). Ask for specifics.

Soft drinks: Allergen-free.
