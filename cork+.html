Our Story

A corner of
the village green
made for lingering

Cork was born from a simple belief: that great wine shouldn't require a passport or a degree — just good company, an open mind, and somewhere comfortable to sit.

Housed in Suffolk House on Wooburn Green's historic village green, we've built a space that feels like a natural extension of the community around it. Our wine list leans proudly Italian — every bottle chosen because we'd happily drink it ourselves.

On the food front, we keep things honest and Italian-inspired. Proper taglieri of cured meats and cheeses from all over Italy, freshly made focacce, and a handful of small plates designed to keep you at the table just a little longer.
